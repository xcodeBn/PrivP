# How to Delete Your Account in Placemap Lebanon

To delete your account in **Placemap Lebanon**, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the App**: Open the Placemap Lebanon application on your device.
2. **Log In**: Sign into your account with your registered email and password.
3. **Go to Settings**: Navigate to the settings menu, usually found by clicking on an icon like a cog or from the main menu.
4. **Account**: Within settings, find and tap on the 'Account' option.
5. **Delete Account**: Look for an option labeled 'Delete Account'. Click on it and follow the prompts to confirm the deletion of your account.

**Please note:** Deleting your account is irreversible and will remove all your data from our servers.

---

**Link to Request Account Deletion:**

If you prefer not to delete your account through the app or if you are unable to log in, you can request account deletion by sending an email to [pisceshelpcenter@outlook.com](mailto:pisceshelpcenter@outlook.com). Please include:

- Your registered email address
- A clear request to delete your account
- YOU MUST EMAIL US FROM THE SAME EMAIL YOU SIGNED UP WITH, A DELETION LINK WILL THEN BE SENT TO YOU
