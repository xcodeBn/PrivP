

**Privacy Policy - CoreCapture**

Last Updated: May 27, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how CoreCapture ("the App") operates with respect to user data. We are committed to providing a transparent and secure experience.

### Our Commitment to Your Privacy

CoreCapture is designed with your privacy as its core principle. We want to be absolutely clear:

* **No Data Collection:** The App does not collect any personal data, usage data, or any other information about you or your device.
* **No Internet Connection:** The App does not connect to the internet. All screen recordings are stored locally on your device.
* **No Ads:** The App is completely free and does not contain any advertisements.
* **No Third-Party Access:** We do not share any information with third parties because we do not collect any information to begin with.

### Information We Do Not Collect

To reiterate, CoreCapture does not collect:

* **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)** such as your name, email address, phone number, or device identifiers.
* **Usage data, crash logs, or analytics.**
* **Screen recordings themselves.** These are stored on your device and are solely under your control.
* Any other information that could be used to identify you or your device.

### Permissions Required

For the App to function correctly, it requires the following permissions:

* **"Display over other apps" / "Draw over other apps" (or similar):** This permission is necessary to display the recording controls and indicators on top of other applications, allowing you to start, pause, and stop recordings from within the App.
* **"Notifications":** This permission is required to display ongoing recording notifications, providing a quick way to manage your recordings and indicating when recording is active.
* **"Microphone":** If you choose to record audio during your screen recording, this permission is required to capture sound from your device's microphone. You have full control over whether to enable audio recording.
* **"Storage" / "Files and Media" (or similar):** This permission is necessary to save your screen recordings to your device's internal or external storage.

**Important Note:** These permissions are used solely for the functionality of the screen recording feature and are not used for data collection or transmission.

### How Your Recordings Are Handled

All screen recordings created with CoreCapture are stored locally on your device. You have full control over these files. We do not have access to, nor do we store, any of your recordings. You are responsible for managing, backing up, and deleting your recordings as you see fit.

### Children's Privacy

CoreCapture does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. As we do not collect any data, the provisions of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) are not applicable.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
